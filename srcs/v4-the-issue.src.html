The Knowing · Issue 08 · Page 03The profile

There is a species of child who receives a working radio and, within the hour, presents the family with a tray of parts. Anhad was that child, in Punjab, and the radios never fully recovered. The habit was not destruction; it was an early form of due diligence.[1] Things worked, and that was precisely the problem — why did they work? Nobody would say. The screwdriver would.

The adult version of this habit is called product management, and it took a detour to get there. In January 2023 he surfaced in Canada, running operations for Quesada — brand strategy with executive leadership in a market he had never lived in, monthly market research, competitive benchmarking, and a rebuild of the customer-experience processes that moved satisfaction indicators up twenty percent. The lesson he kept: a till queue is a funnel, and funnels can be redesigned.[2]

Then came Zen Den Homes, luxury short-term rentals, where he ran growth and operations for two years and the numbers stopped being polite. Fourteen listings became forty-plus. Revenue rose seventy-eight percent year on year. Dynamic pricing lifted occupancy thirty percent. He wrote the onboarding workflow and the SOPs himself — ramp-up got thirty percent faster, issue resolution sixty percent faster — because the fastest way to fix an operation is to treat it as an unshipped product.

Since May 2026 he has been at Blostem, a Zerodha-Rainmatter-backed B2B fintech infrastructure company, where his title says product and his output says product and design. He owns both for Jaano — RBI-compliant video KYC and video personal discussion, three verification models shipped: officer-led, self-serve, and AI avatar-guided — for which he designed the customer journeys, the admin, agent and auditor consoles, and the design system they all run on.[3] And for VoiceBrew, AI voice agents for banks and lenders, he took campaign scheduling and outcome-based sequencing from zero to one and turned each client's collections, card-activation and loan-outreach processes into call flows a machine can follow and a human can defend.

Ask him for the philosophy and you get one sentence, delivered like a house rule — the same rule that governs every AI feature he ships. It is the radio child, grown up: the machine may be clever, but somebody still has to hold the screwdriver.

“AI signals advise, a human decides.”

  • No customer dead-ends. Ever. Every journey has a way forward.
