CONTRIBUTION SHEET · SC.04

Jaano — product & design owner

RBI-regulated video verification. My job: make a compliance product that banks trust and humans can actually finish.

  • Discovery with the people who live in it: auditors, underwriters, compliance, operations.
  • Defined, prioritized and shipped three verification models — officer-led, self-serve, AI avatar-guided.
  • Designed the customer journeys and the admin, agent and auditor consoles, plus the design system they all run on.
  • Defined the reporting suite: one case report + eight ops reports, shaped with underwriters.
  • Product rules I fought for: AI signals advise, a human decides · no customer ever dead-ends · every checklist, language and journey is tenant-configurable — that configurability became the lead USP.
  • Wrote the positioning and buyer decks that took it to banks; every claim verified line-by-line against production code.

Product screens live behind NDAs. The thinking doesn't. Ask me anything: anhadsb2@gmail.com
